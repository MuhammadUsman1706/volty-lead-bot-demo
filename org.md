HOW IT WORKS: learns from the website, documents, price lists and FAQs and asks the team about gaps first. Starts in draft mode (staff approve replies); go live per inbox, can switch by business hours. Pauses 30 min in a chat after a staff reply. Corrections become Learned rules with undo. Risky actions (broadcasts, deletions) need admin approval. Multilingual, remembers each contact's language. Web chat uses cards (product carousel, time slots, forms, choices, order status, tables, fact lists). WhatsApp coexistence keeps the WhatsApp Business app on the same number; imports up to 6 months of history. 270+ integrations on every plan (HubSpot, Salesforce, Shopify, Google Calendar, Calendly, Sheets, Notion, Slack, Xero, Stripe, clinic systems, any MCP server or API).

SECURITY: AWS Singapore, Cloudflare, AES-256 at rest, TLS in transit, row-level isolation, passwordless sign-in, CASA Tier 2, CSA Cyber Essentials, PDPA-aligned, SOC 2 Type II in progress. Phones, emails and card numbers tokenised before the AI reads them; zero data retention on AI calls; never used to train models.
